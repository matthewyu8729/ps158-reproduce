# Rebound Lab

An interactive research model exploring when AI productivity gains could increase total human labour hours.

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step or account is required. Sliders change productivity, AI adoption, labour cost share, AI costs, price pass-through, demand elasticity, automation, background demand and US work retention.

The model includes software development, graphic design and customer-service presets, live equations, a sensitivity chart, and JSON export. Scenario inputs are explicitly labelled; results are conditional illustrations, not forecasts.
